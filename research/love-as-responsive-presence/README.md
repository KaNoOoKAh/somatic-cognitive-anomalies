# Love as Responsive Presence

This folder collects notes, reflections, and research materials about the idea that love and affection can be expressed through attention, responsiveness, care, and meaningful change in any form.

## Contents
- `manifesto.md` — core philosophical statement
- `principles.md` — working definitions and framing
- `poem.md` — lyrical version of the idea
- `questions.md` — research and reflection prompts
- `dialogue.md` — the conversation prompt and response section
