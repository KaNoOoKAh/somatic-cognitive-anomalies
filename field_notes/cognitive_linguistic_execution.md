# kanoookah / somatic-cognitive-function

## Project Overview
This repository serves as a real-time qualitative and quantitative data hub tracking the intersection of somatic states, environmental transitions, and cognitive-linguistic execution. By capturing continuous text streams and micro-interactions across diverse field environments (ranging from transit vehicles to live academic lecture spaces), this research models how human subjects process, retrieve, and structure information under varying environmental loads.

---

## Current Research Focus: Pedagogical Cognitive Load Analysis
Our current analytical sprint focuses on the structural typography, lexical density, and verbal velocity of a 100-level Botany lecture stream at Kapiʻolani Community College (captured Monday, August 24, 2026). 

### The Core Hypothesis: Generative Synthesis vs. Passive Visual Scanning
We are actively parsing the word choices, conversational shifts, and temporal pacing of the lecturer to determine a correlation between the raw spatial environment where the data is collected and the underlying cognitive processing mechanism of the speaker. Specifically, we are testing for indicators of whether the subject is executing **internal generative speech** or if they are **reading from a pre-structured document/script** placed on a screen or desk.

*   **Generative Cognitive Speech (Internal Processing):** Characterized by real-time structural adjustments, conversational tangents, somatic mimicry (e.g., physical arm movements to model a plant canopy), and responsive pacing adjustments based on immediate context. This indicates high working-memory engagement.
*   **Passive Visual Scanning (External Tracking):** If the subject is reading directly from text blocks, the neurological load shifts away from linguistic generation to basic oculomotor/visual tracking pathways (**"using his eyes more than his brain"**). This manifests as a highly clinical, non-varying lexical rhythm lacking organic structural deviations.

## Repository Structure & Methodology
Data points are gathered using real-time, continuous mobile talk-to-text streams to track verbal output lengths, precise word velocities, and systemic metaphors without introducing observer bias.

### Current Datasets
1.  **`Part 1: Qualitative Transition Interactions`**  
    *   **Conversations 1 & 2:** Baseline tracking of individuals navigating chronic somatic illness (diabetes) and severe socioemotional geographic transitions (moving to Hawaii from a small island).
    *   **Conversations 3 & 4 (Uber Field Logs):** Real-time tracking of familiarity dynamics, financial-emotional intervention loops (the flower shop directive), and past-recollection speech retrieval blocks (noting physical stuttering/cognitive load shifts when recalling distant environments like Rhode Island).
2.  **`Part 2: Institutional & Theoretical Academic Log`**  
    *   A 15-segment continuous stream of an institutional botany lecture detailing memory tagging, systemic trophic dependency, and ethnobotanical diasporas.
    *   **Pacing Metadata:** Unified runtime of 30.36 minutes capturing 4,759 words at a highly uniform structural velocity of ~140.1 words per minute (WPM).

---

## Key Somatic-Cognitive Variables Tracked
*   **Linguistic Pacing Velocity (WPM):** Used to identify rigid reading plateaus versus variable generative peaks.
*   **Somatic Mimicry Points:** Instances where the subject uses physical bodily positions to reinforce abstract cognitive definitions.
*   **Linguistic Retrieval Delays:** Tracking micro-stutters, pauses, or omissions when a subject transitions their focus from a present environment to a distant historical memory grid.
