## Section 5: Somatic Activation Challenge (Optional Experiment)

> To help us measure how real-time physical adjustments impact cognitive clarity, choose **one** of the short physical interventions below to practice over the next 1–2 hours:

- [ ] **Option A: Light-Footed / Barefoot Walking**  
  *Task:* Practice walking softly, placing your feet gently ground-to-heel or forefoot-first without heavy heel strikes, for 10–15 minutes.
- [ ] **Option B: Posture & Interoceptive Reset**  
  *Task:* Every 20 minutes, perform 5-7 deep nasal breaths while keeping your shoulders dropped and jaw uncluttered.
- [ ] **Option C: Control Group**  
  *Task:* Maintain your usual routine without changing your movement patterns.

**Note for Follow-Up:** Pay attention to any shifts in your mental clarity, tension, energy, or focus while doing this task. You will report what happened (or didn't happen) in the follow-up survey.
