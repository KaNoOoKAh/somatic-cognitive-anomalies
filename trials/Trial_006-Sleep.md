## Objective & Protocol
To execute a systematic, voluntary downregulation of the peripheral nervous system prior to sleep sleep-onset. The protocol involves consciously guiding the upper and lower extremities into an absolute state of relaxation—simulating a local "shut down" or profound de-excitation of motor and sensory tone—and tracking the subsequent somatic feedback.

## Trial Parameters & Tracking Metrics
- **Voluntary Motor Cessation:** Assessing the ease or resistance of entirely releasing muscular tension in the limbs sequentially (e.g., distal to proximal).
- **Interoceptive Feedback at Rest:** Documenting whether the "shut down" state induces localized sensations (such as profound heaviness, weightlessness, tingling, or temperature shifts) or if it initiates systemic sensory propagation similar to previous trials.
- **Transition Dynamics:** Monitoring how this profound appendicular relaxation interacts with the conscious transition into sleep, noting any sudden involuntary motor jerks, sensory spikes, or cognitive shifts.
