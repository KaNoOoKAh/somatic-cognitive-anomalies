- **Cephalic Infiltration:** Of note, the sensory wave traveled superiorly beyond the spinal cord, directly reaching the neck, head, and facial regions. 

## Functional Implications
The transition from a restricted, localized dorsal pathway to an all-inclusive, systemic sensation—extending explicitly to the face and head—points to a profound shift in how visceral reflexes (like the defecation reflex) are broadcasting across the broader autonomic and somatosensory networks under low-strain conditions.
